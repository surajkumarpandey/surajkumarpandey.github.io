About

I am a researcher and engineer focused on designing efficient and adaptive Artificial Intelligence systems for resource-constrained environments, particularly in Cyber-Physical Systems and edge devices. My work intersects bio-inspired algorithms, embodied intelligence, and real-world learning, with emphasis on handling noisy, non-stationary data through techniques such as neuroevolution, domain adaptation, and continual learning.
