Research Statement

My research focuses on designing efficient and adaptive Artificial Intelligence systems for resource-constrained environments, particularly in Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) and edge devices. I work at the intersection of bio-inspired algorithms, embodied intelligence, and real-world learning, with emphasis on handling noisy, non-stationary data through techniques such as neuroevolution, domain adaptation, and continual learning.

Future Research Directions

  • Continual and lifelong learning for edge AI systems
  • Bio-inspired and neuroevolutionary methods for adaptive intelligence
  • Efficient deployment of deep learning and LLMs on resource-constrained devices
  • Domain adaptation and sim-to-real transfer in robotics
  • Embodied AI for autonomous systems in real-world CPS environments
